# Source routing

These are discovery entrypoints, not proof that a release is current. Open the actual dated release and recheck links/schedules each run. Publisher pages can block automated retrieval; use the skill's qualified secondary status when necessary.

| Report | Publisher entrypoint | Check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Michigan sentiment and inflation expectations | https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/ and https://data.sca.isr.umich.edu/ | Page heading, survey month, preliminary versus final, and next-release notice. Follow Reports/Tables for omitted detail. |
| S&P Global flash/final PMIs | https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Release/ReleaseDates and https://www.pmi.spglobal.com/Public/Release/PressReleases | Country, flash/final, manufacturing/services/composite, publication date. Calendar is usually UTC; convert with date-aware zones. Search the official spglobal.com domain if the PMI host is blocked. |
| CPI, PPI, payrolls, JOLTS, ECI | https://www.bls.gov/schedule/ | Current monthly schedule and dated news release; employment and inflation revisions. |
| GDP, personal income/outlays, PCE | https://www.bea.gov/news/schedule/ | Advance/second/third estimate, monthly versus annualized/YoY, headline/core. |
| Retail sales, durable goods, housing, trade | https://www.census.gov/economic-indicators/ | Release period, preliminary/revised, adjusted/unadjusted, and headline/exclusion measures. |
| Weekly jobless claims | https://www.dol.gov/ui/data.pdf | Date and week ending, initial/continuing, seasonal adjustment, revised prior week. |
| ISM manufacturing/services | https://www.ismworld.org/supply-management-news-and-reports/reports/ism-report-on-business/ | Manufacturing/services distinction, headline, prices, employment, new orders. |
| Conference Board confidence / LEI | https://www.conference-board.org/topics/consumer-confidence and https://www.conference-board.org/topics/us-leading-indicators | Publication date and reference month; do not confuse confidence with Michigan sentiment. |
| Fed/FOMC | https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/calendar.htm and https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm | Statement, decision, projections, minutes, and press conference have separate timings. |
| EIA energy data | https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/ and https://www.eia.gov/naturalgas/weekly/ | Holiday-adjusted schedule and report week. |
| Baker Hughes rigs | https://rigcount.bakerhughes.com/ | Weekly North America versus monthly international, count date, oil/gas/total. Lower priority for broad equities; relevant for energy. |

Use a current financial calendar for event discovery and consensus, such as MarketWatch's economic calendar (https://www.marketwatch.com/economy-politics/calendar), Investing.com's economic calendar (https://www.investing.com/economic-calendar/), or an accessible financial institution's published survey table. Cite the specific calendar page and retrieval time, not this list. Calendar timezones may be personalized or ambiguous. Verify the timezone before conversion; prefer the publisher if schedules conflict.

Use timestamped Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, or another reputable financial wire/outlet as a secondary result source where accessible. Search engines may surface old pages under current-looking snippets. Verify the article body, date, period, vintage, and actual-versus-forecast columns. If no fresh source is accessible, report the gap instead of inferring a result.
