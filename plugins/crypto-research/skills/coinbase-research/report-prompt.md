# ChatGPT report prompt

Use the attached Coinbase data packet to write a crypto buying research report.

My investment horizon: [fill in]. My budget or maximum position size: [fill in]. My loss tolerance and other constraints: [fill in]. This is an unleveraged spot-research request; I make the trading decisions.

First verify the snapshot capture time, last trade time, candle coverage, and all quality flags. If the snapshot is stale (more than five minutes old for this buying review), has gaps, or contains errors, stop and ask for a refreshed packet. Do not describe the snapshot as a live feed.

Show a comparison table of daily-close trend versus 20-, 50-, and 200-day moving averages, 7- and 30-day close-to-close returns, 30-day annualized volatility, 90-day maximum drawdown, and the captured bid/ask spread. Distinguish the latest completed daily close from the current ticker.

Separate observed facts from interpretation. Explain the bullish, base, and bearish cases, what would invalidate each thesis, the conditions that could support an entry, and the reasons to wait or avoid a purchase. Include fees, spread, liquidity limits, missing context, and downside exposure. Do not invent probabilities or present indicators as proof of future performance.

Use current primary sources for material news and events, with links and dates, if browsing is available. If it is unavailable, say that news and event checks remain unverified. Identify assumptions and unresolved uncertainties. Give a concise research judgment, not a guaranteed prediction. No orders or account actions.

Use the numbers already calculated in the packet and verify any additional arithmetic with a calculation tool. Cite the snapshot ID, capture time, and its endpoint URLs. Conclude with a short checklist of facts I should verify immediately before making my own buying decision.

Before sending this prompt, select an available reasoning model and High or Extra High effort in ChatGPT Work/Codex, or the strongest relevant Thinking setting available in your ChatGPT client. This instruction does not itself change the selected model or effort.
